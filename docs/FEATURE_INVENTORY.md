# Feature Inventory - Visible in Intus Examples

Detailed per-page extraction lives in `docs/example_analysis/`.

## Global (all pages)

- Sticky purple header with Intus logo.
- Top navigation links.
- Global search field with clear action.
- Locale and currency selector.
- Wishlist entry point.
- Shopping cart entry point.
- Sign-in / user menu.
- Mobile menu.
- Footer with informational, help, partner, social, and legal links.
- Newsletter subscription.
- Cookie settings.
- App promotion banner.
- Payment method trust badges.

- "Popular add-ons" block.

- "Most popular games" block.
- "Most popular e-gift cards" block.
- "Most popular add-ons" block.
- "Pre-order" block.
- Category accordion with platform, genre, niche genre, skins, and price groups.

## Product page

References: `docs/example_analysis/GTA_VI_page_examples.md`, `docs/example_analysis/PEAK_page_example.md`, `docs/example_analysis/robux_page_example.md`

- Breadcrumb navigation.
- Product title, cover, platform, region.
- Price and discount information.
- Buy button and add-to-cart button.
- Recommended offers.
